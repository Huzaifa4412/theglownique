# Pricing – The Glownique Custom Signage

The Glownique offers bespoke illuminated signage. All quotes are customized based on dimensions, materials, and complexity. We provide a **100% free true-to-scale mockup and quote within ~2 hours** of submitting a request.

## Custom LED Neon Signs
- **Price Range:** Starts from $150 (varies heavily based on size and complexity)
- **Use Case:** Weddings, home decor, aesthetic storefronts, indoor branding
- **Includes:** Clear acrylic backing (cut to shape or square), commercial-grade 12V silicone LED neon, power supply, remote dimmer, mounting hardware
- **Warranty:** 5 years

## 3D Metal Channel Letter Signs
- **Price Range:** Custom Quote Only (Requires vector logo and dimensions)
- **Use Case:** Outdoor corporate branding, building facades, premium indoor reception areas
- **Configurations:**
  - Front-Lit (Face illumination)
  - Halo-Lit / Back-Lit (Wall wash illumination)
  - Dual-Lit (Face and Wall illumination)
- **Material:** Precision-fabricated 304 stainless steel and commercial-grade LEDs
- **Warranty:** 5 years

## Ultra-Thin Lightboxes
- **Price Range:** Starts from $250
- **Use Case:** Restaurant menus, retail point-of-sale displays, exhibitions
- **Features:** Edge-lit slim aluminum frame, magnetic tool-free graphic swap system

## 3D UV Printed Acrylic Signs
- **Price Range:** Custom Quote Only
- **Use Case:** High-detail multicolored brand logos combined with neon lighting
- **Features:** High-definition UV printing directly on acrylic, optionally contoured with LED neon accents

### Shipping
Global, tracked, and insured delivery. Large architectural signs are shipped in secure timber crates.
