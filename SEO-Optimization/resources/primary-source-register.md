## Research method and limitations

- TinyFish US English search captured SERP observations; its result order is a dated public observation, not a universal ranking.
- TinyFish fetch read current primary and competitor pages. Pages can change after access.
- A broad TinyFish browser run did not reliably surface head/schema fields; targeted rendered-head fetches corrected the metadata record. JSON-LD findings therefore use repository source and must be rendered/validated during implementation.
- Authenticated GSC, Bing, analytics, paid SEO metrics and server logs were unavailable.
- External skill badges were not independently audited; they describe what the listing displayed.
