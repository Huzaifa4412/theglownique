# Six- and Twelve-Month Roadmap

## Months 4–6: validate and deepen

Target: approximately 48 total high-quality pages only where GSC, buyer conversations and proof support expansion.

### Priorities

1. Refresh the first 90-day portfolio using query, CTR, index and conversion evidence.
2. Complete deferred channel-letter pricing, materials/lighting and process/QC pages.
3. Expand the highest-performing industry/product intersections with substantive case studies—not templated variations.
4. Launch or improve one defensible tool: viewing-distance calculator, cost estimator or installation checklist.
5. Earn relevant editorial, supplier, association and industry links.
6. Improve organic qualified-inquiry rate and handoff attribution.
7. Pursue top-ten long-tail commercial topics before broad `custom neon signs`.

### Decision rules

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Indexed, impressions growing, relevant queries, weak CTR | Improve title/value proposition and snippet-content match; do not add a duplicate page. |
| Traffic grows, qualified conversions weak | Strengthen proof, decision support and CTA; validate audience/intent. |
| Two pages overlap queries | Consolidate, differentiate purpose or redirect the weaker page. |
| No impressions and no validated demand after adequate time | Reassess intent/source demand; merge or retire rather than refresh forever. |
| AI citations appear but recommendations favor competitors | Build third-party consensus, verified reviews and differentiated proof; do not chase more self-citations alone. |
| One product/industry cluster produces qualified leads | Deepen case studies, guide coverage and relevant outreach around it. |

### Month 6 exit criteria

- Technical release suite is routine and zero unintended routes are indexable.
- Current claims have evidence owners and monthly validation.
- Content refresh decisions use owned data.
- Relevant referring domains and assisted conversions are reported with context.
- At least one linkable asset has real usage/outreach evidence.
- The program can identify which clusters create qualified inquiries, not only impressions.

## Months 7–12: consolidate authority

### Priorities

- Consolidate topical authority around the product/industry combinations that generate qualified leads.
- Publish deeper project datasets, technical comparisons, video/process evidence and advanced tools.
- Build recurring expert commentary with relevant trade and business publications.
- Improve media search surfaces with original images, captions and eligible video data.
- Mature entity identity and review/profile consistency.
- Improve sales attribution from first discovery through Etsy sale.
- Retire or merge low-value pages to protect quality.

### International expansion gate

Do not launch country/language folders until:

- US performance demonstrates a repeatable conversion model;
- fulfillment, pricing, duties, warranty and legal policies are localized;
- native-quality content and local keyword research are funded;
- hreflang, self-canonicals and reciprocal locale mapping can be maintained;
- each locale has unique, useful content—not translated boilerplate.

### Commerce evolution gate

Reconsider Merchant Center, direct checkout, hybrid catalog feeds and agentic-commerce protocols only if the sales model changes.

Direct Merchant Center/merchant-listing readiness requires:

- directly purchasable products/configurations on a page;
- stable visible price, currency and availability;
- accurate shipping/returns data;
- consistent site/feed/schema facts;
- operational capacity to maintain the feed.

Quote-led pages should not masquerade as direct offers to access richer surfaces.

## Twelve-month outcomes

By month 12 the program should be able to show, with baselines:

- intended index coverage and median time to valid/indexed status;
- non-brand commercial impressions/clicks and top-ten query/page distribution;
- qualified organic inquiry, Etsy handoff and attributable-sale trends;
- which content clusters and proof assets assist revenue;
- relevant referring domains and earned-mention quality;
- AI citation, mention and recommendation rates by tracked prompt set;
- content refresh/consolidation decisions and claim accuracy.

No target is considered achieved solely because a page count, backlink count or AI citation count increased.

## Budget allocation principle

For a moderate authority budget, fund in this order:

1. measurement, technical trust and claims accuracy;
2. expert/proof production and content design;
3. one genuinely useful tool or dataset;
4. targeted outreach and relationship building;
5. paid platform data for keyword/backlink validation;
6. experimental AI-only protocols after foundational work.
