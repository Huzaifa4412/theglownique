# Digital PR Outreach Templates

## Pre-send qualification

- Recipient/publication:
- Audience relevance:
- Recent relevant article/resource:
- Why this asset/project helps that audience:
- Proposed source/asset URL:
- Relationship or product value to disclose:
- Desired outcome (editorial consideration, expert source, resource addition—not guaranteed link/rank):
- Owner/follow-up date:

Do not send if relevance is weak, the evidence is not public-ready, rights are unclear or the request depends on positive coverage/exact anchor/followed link.

## Expert data/resource pitch

Subject: Source for your [specific topic]: [asset or finding]

Hi [Name],

Your [article/resource] on [specific detail] is useful for [their audience]. We have just published [plain description of original chart, calculator, dataset or comparison] based on [method/evidence]. It helps a buyer answer [specific decision].

The most useful finding is [one accurate, non-promotional finding]. The methodology and limitations are here: [URL]. I can also provide [original image/data/expert comment] with attribution.

If it is useful for a future update, please use whatever wording and destination best serves your readers. No placement or positive coverage is expected.

[Name, relevant role/expertise]  
[Canonical site/contact]

Disclosure: [product supplied / collaboration / none].

## Case-study pitch

Subject: A [industry] signage project shaped by [interesting constraint]

Hi [Name],

We documented a recent [sign type] project for [customer type] where the key constraint was [specific challenge]. The useful lesson was [technical/business trade-off], not simply the finished appearance.

The case includes [measurements/materials/process/outcome/media] with customer permission: [URL]. [Named expert] can explain [specific topic] or provide original images.

Would this be relevant to your coverage of [topic]? No positive treatment or link is required; I am sharing it because the project provides [audience value].

[Signature and disclosure]

## Partner/supplier request

Subject: Accurate project/resource reference for [shared material/product]

Hi [Name],

We used [verified supplier/material/relationship] in [project/resource] and documented [specific application]. Your audience/resource page may benefit from the implementation example: [URL].

If you maintain a partner/example/resource section, would you review it for inclusion? We can provide confirmation, specifications and approved media. Please use your normal editorial criteria; no exact anchor or followed link is requested.

[Signature]

## Honest product-review invitation

Subject: Would you independently evaluate [specific product/use case]?

Hi [Name],

We can provide [product/value] for an independent evaluation relevant to your [audience]. There is no requirement to publish, link, recommend us or express a positive view. Any coverage must clearly disclose the supplied product/relationship, and you should retain editorial control.

If the fit is appropriate, I can send specifications, limitations, logistics and disclosure details before you decide.

[Signature]

## Follow-up (once)

Hi [Name],

One follow-up in case [asset/project] is useful for your [specific audience/topic]: [URL]. The concise takeaway is [finding]. If it is not a fit, no reply is needed and I will close the loop.

Thanks,  
[Name]
