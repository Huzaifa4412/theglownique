## Customer and challenge

1. What type of business/project was this?
2. What space, audience and viewing conditions mattered?
3. What problem was the sign meant to solve?
4. What options had the customer considered?
5. What constraints existed: wall, dimensions, landlord, power, weather, deadline, brand guide, budget, shipping or permits?
6. What would failure have looked like?

## Design decision

1. Why was this sign type chosen?
2. Which alternative was rejected, and why?
3. How was the logo/artwork translated into the sign?
4. What changed during mockup/revisions?
5. How were colors/finishes approved?
6. What trade-off did the customer make among price, visibility, depth, durability, portability and installation?

## Outcome

1. What changed after installation/use?
2. What did the customer or their audience notice?
3. Was any outcome measured? State method, dates and baseline.
4. Did the customer reorder, refer or deploy across locations?
5. What would they do differently?

Never convert qualitative feedback into a percentage.
