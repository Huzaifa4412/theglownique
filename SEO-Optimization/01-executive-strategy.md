# Executive Strategy

## Decision

Position The Glownique as the expert national supplier of custom illuminated business signage—not merely another custom-neon store—then earn visibility with technically eligible pages, first-party project evidence, transparent buying guidance and credible third-party mentions.

The conversion path remains:

`organic or AI discovery → commercial/guide/case-study page → free mockup or quote → WhatsApp conversation → Etsy payment → attributable sale`

This is a national ecommerce/export model. It is not a US local-business SEO program.

## Strategic choices

1. **B2B first.** Build `/business-signs` and its product, industry, guide and proof layers before expanding consumer/event topics.
2. **Commercial pages before volume publishing.** The first four destination pages correspond to logo neon, channel letters, lightboxes and acrylic logo signs.
3. **Proof before prose scale.** Every page must include real specifications, images, constraints, process evidence, pricing context or documented outcomes.
4. **Long-tail first.** Pursue achievable product/use-case and comparison queries before the broad `custom neon signs` head term.
5. **No local doorway strategy.** A national supplier must not fabricate local presence.
6. **Separate citation from conversion.** AI mention/citation tracking complements—but never replaces—qualified inquiry and revenue measurement.
7. **Refresh based on evidence.** After 90 days, update or consolidate pages using GSC and conversion data instead of continuously adding URLs.

## Guardrails against false certainty

- No ranking guarantee or fabricated deadline for page-one results.
- No invented volume, difficulty, conversion rate, backlink count or AI citation benchmark.
- `site:` results are diagnostic observations only.
- “90% valid index coverage within 14 days” is a service-level target, not a promise that search engines will index every page.
- Product prices, warranties, ratings, lead times and delivery claims require owner evidence and visible-site consistency.
- Third-party skill statistics and SERPs are dated snapshots.

## Definition of success

The program succeeds when all four conditions improve together:

1. intended pages are crawlable, canonical and indexed;
2. non-brand commercial visibility expands across the approved B2B clusters;
3. trusted search and AI systems cite or mention useful Glownique evidence;
4. organic discovery produces qualified quote starts, Etsy handoffs and attributable sales at an improving rate.
