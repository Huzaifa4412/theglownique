## 2. Competitive Content Gaps: Where Competitors Are Vulnerable

A granular audit of competitor blogs and resource hubs (*Yellowpop, Custom Neon, ShineNeon, AhaNeon, NeonChamp*) revealed four systemic weaknesses in their organic search footprints:

1. **Anonymous, Cookie-Cutter Content (E-E-A-T Void):**  
   Competitor articles on topics like *"How long do neon signs last"* or *"LED vs Glass"* are ghostwritten without engineer bylines, published dates, or peer-reviewed citations. Google's Search Quality Rater Guidelines prioritize content with demonstrated firsthand experience.
2. **The "Permit & Compliance" Black Hole:**  
   B2B buyers (especially storefronts, restaurants, and retail chains) fear code violations, landlord rejections, and fire safety penalties. Not a single leading illuminated neon specialist explains US municipal signage regulations, UL 48 listings, or California Title 24 compliance.
3. **Missing Quantitative Benchmarks & Calculators:**  
   Most sites feature vanity mood boards without actionable engineering tools. There is huge unmet demand for letter height vs. viewing distance calculators, power draw cost formulas, and lumen output comparisons.
4. **Zero Google News / Freshness Signals (QDF):**  
   Competitors treat their sites as static catalogs. They miss the massive search spikes driven by **Query Deserves Freshness (QDF)** and **Google Discover** regarding commercial interior design trends, municipal lighting bans, and energy efficiency mandates.

## 5. Technical Implementation & Quality Gate Contract

Every newly created news or commercial route must satisfy the repository's strict quality rules:

1. **Route Manifest Registration (`lib/routes.ts`):**  
   Every new page must be registered with an explicit `lastModified`, `priority`, and `changeFrequency`.
2. **SEO Regression Suite (`npm run seo:audit`):**  
   Pages must pass `scripts/seo-audit.mjs` with 0 failures:
   - `<title>` length ≤ 60 characters with decoding.
   - Distinct canonical pointing to production origin.
   - Zero retired claim references (no expired free-delivery promotions).
   - Valid, sanitized JSON-LD structured data.
3. **IndexNow Instant Ping (`npm run indexnow`):**  
   Whenever a new news or commercial page is published, submit the URL payload to IndexNow to trigger immediate crawling by Bing, Yandex, and participating search engines.
4. **Sanity Studio Publishing:**  
   News articles authored in Sanity must include a named, verified author, reviewer, reading time, direct answer summary, and explicit source citations.
