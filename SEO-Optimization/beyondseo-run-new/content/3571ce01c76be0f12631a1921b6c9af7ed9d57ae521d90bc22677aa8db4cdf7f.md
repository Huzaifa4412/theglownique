# Custom Business Sign Cost Guide

Understand the core factors that influence custom illuminated signage pricing — sign category, dimensions, illumination method, acrylic thickness, and outdoor weatherproofing.

## What Determines the Price of a Custom Business Sign?

Because custom business signs are engineered to order based on your brand logo, physical wall dimensions, and illumination specifications, pricing varies across projects. Knowing the key cost drivers helps you optimize your signage budget while achieving maximum brand impact.

| Sign Category | Primary Application | Key Cost Drivers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Custom LED Neon Signs | Brand Walls, Reception, Bar/Lounge | Total tube length, letter height, acrylic backer cut |
| 3D Metal Channel Letters | Storefront Façades, Building Exteriors | Stainless grade, illumination type (front/halo/dual) |
| Ultra-Thin Lightboxes | Retail Display, Menus, Window Signs | Frame size, single/double sided, edge-lit LED density |
| 3D Acrylic UV Logo Signs | Corporate Offices, Salons, Reception | Layer count, UV print area, neon contour complexity |

### Key Cost Factors Explained

#### 1. Physical Dimensions & Scale

Larger signs require wider acrylic backers, additional structural bracing, and a higher density of LED modules and power transformers.

#### 2. Illumination Method & LEDs

Single-color LED neon is cost-effective, while RGB color-changing LEDs or dual-lit (front-lit + rear halo) 3D metal letters require dual wiring runs and advanced controllers.

#### 3. Indoor vs. Outdoor Weatherproofing (IP67)

Exterior storefront signs require IP67 sealed silicone tubing, waterproof transformers, and weather-resistant backing materials to withstand rain, wind, and UV exposure.

### Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

#### How much does a custom neon sign cost?

Small to medium custom LED neon signs (approx 20–36 inches) typically range from $120 to $350. Large commercial feature wall signs (40–80+ inches) range from $400 to $900+ depending on total tubing length, font complexity, and acrylic cut style.

#### What is the average price of 3D metal channel letter signs for storefronts?

Fabricated commercial channel letter signs typically range from $800 to $3,500+ depending on letter height, stainless steel grade (304 vs 316), illumination style (frontlit, halo backlit, or dual-lit), and raceway vs flush mounting.

#### Why is a 3D acrylic UV-print sign more economical for complex logos?

When a logo has multiple gradient colors, small tagline text, or intricate emblems, rendering it purely in hand-bent neon tubing requires dozens of manual solder joints and metres of silicone tube. With UV-print acrylic, the printer produces high-definition detail in one pass, allowing neon to be placed only along key glowing contours — cutting build time and cost by 30–50%.
