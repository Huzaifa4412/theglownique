## Commercial Lightbox FAQs

### How thin is an ultra-thin LED lightbox sign?

Our slim commercial lightboxes have an overall profile under 1 inch (~25mm) deep. They sit virtually flush against interior walls or suspend cleanly in storefront window displays without bulky enclosures.

### How does edge-lit LED illumination prevent hotspots?

We mount high-density SMD LEDs along the precision-extruded aluminum frame, directing light into an optical-grade acrylic light-guide panel (LGP) with micro-laser etched dot matrices for 100% uniform, shadow-free backlighting.

### How do you change promotional graphics or restaurant menus?

Our lightboxes feature tool-free front-loading snap frames or magnetic front covers. Simply flip open the four edges, insert a new backlit film poster, and snap the frame shut in under 60 seconds.

### Can lightbox signs be mounted double-sided in storefront windows?

Yes. We offer both single-sided wall-mount models and double-sided ceiling-hung lightboxes equipped with aircraft cable suspension systems for dual-facing retail window visibility.
