# Entity Authority and Digital PR

## Objective

Make “The Glownique” consistently understood as a custom illuminated business-signage brand and earn relevant third-party evidence that supports both buyer trust and discovery systems.

## Entity foundation

### Canonical identity record

Owner-approved facts must be maintained in one internal record:

- trading and legal name;
- concise category statement;
- canonical domain and logo;
- verified contact and country/fulfillment facts;
- founder/expert names and bios;
- canonical Etsy shop profile;
- Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, YouTube and other genuinely owned profiles;
- product categories and approved terminology;
- warranty, lead-time and fulfillment claims with evidence dates.

Recommended category statement:

> The Glownique designs and manufactures made-to-order illuminated business signage, including custom LED neon logos, metal channel letters, slim lightboxes and illuminated acrylic signs.

Do not claim US manufacturing, an address, a membership, certification or installation network without evidence.

## Profile sequence

1. Correct the Etsy destination from a listing to the canonical shop profile where entity/reference context is intended.
2. Align the site's organization record, Etsy, Instagram and Facebook name/category/description/link.
3. Create Pinterest and YouTube only with a maintained content plan and real assets.
4. Add relevant directories or memberships only where category, geography and editorial standards fit.
5. Record each profile URL, owner, login custodian, last review and exact approved facts.

Profile consistency does not mean copy-pasting identical promotional text. It means the entity, category, domain and core facts agree.

## Proof production

Target two permissioned case studies per month after the initial evidence intake. Use [templates/case-study-interview.md](templates/case-study-interview.md).

Case-study evidence hierarchy:

1. original measurements, photos/video and documented timeline;
2. customer-approved challenge/outcome/testimonial;
3. production/material/QC records;
4. seller/order references that can be verified without exposing personal data;
5. expert explanation of trade-offs.

No outcome should be converted into a percentage without measured before/after data.

## Linkable assets

Prioritize assets that a designer, owner or industry publisher can use:

| Asset | Evidence required | Distribution targets | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Viewing-distance and letter-size chart | Transparent method, expert review, limitations | architects, retail/design publications, small-business resources | Relevant editorial links and tool-assisted quotes |
| Front/halo/dual-lit visual comparison | Same logo/context, accurate labels | sign/design media, agencies, Pinterest/YouTube | Cited comparison page and assisted inquiries |
| Sign cost estimator | Approved cost model, ranges, assumptions, update owner | small-business/retail media | Qualified estimator completions and links |
| Installation readiness checklist | Qualified review, jurisdiction caveat | property/facilities resources | Downloads/use and commercial assists |
| Original project dataset | Permissioned anonymized records and methodology | trade/retail/business media | Earned coverage and citations |

Launch one strong asset first. A calculator without trustworthy data is worse than a sourced guide.

## Prospect tiers

| Tier | Examples | Value proposition | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sign-industry associations, suppliers, fabrication/design publications | Original technical insight, project data, useful tools | Highest relevance; personalize manually |
| 2 | Retail, restaurant, salon, fitness, office-design and event trade media | Industry-specific project/problem evidence | Pitch only where a matching case/assets exist |
| 3 | Business directories and partner/resource pages | Accurate supplier listing or practical resource | Reject low-quality link farms and irrelevant paid placements |
| 4 | Creators/reviewers | Honest product experience and disclosure | No required sentiment, anchor or ranking promise |

## Outreach rules

- Lead with the recipient's audience and a real evidence asset.
- Disclose commercial/product relationships clearly.
- Do not request followed links, exact-match anchors or positive coverage as a condition.
- Product seeding may request honest feedback, never a five-star/positive review.
- Never buy rankings, links, reviews, followers or undisclosed endorsements.
- FTC guidance (`FTC-01`) prohibits incentives conditioned on positive/negative sentiment; incentives and material relationships may require clear disclosure.
- Track declined/removed links and negative feedback as learning, not something to suppress.

Use [templates/digital-pr-outreach.md](templates/digital-pr-outreach.md).

## Authority tracking

For every earned mention/link, record:

- referring URL/domain and publication date;
- target page and anchor/context;
- relationship/relevance category;
- follow/nofollow/sponsored/UGC where visible;
- pitch/campaign and cost/product value;
- disclosure status;
- discovery/index status;
- referral sessions, quote starts and assisted sales;
- removal/quality notes.

Report relevant new referring domains and assisted conversions; do not celebrate raw link count without context.

## 90-day authority cadence

- Weeks 1–2: identity record, profile corrections, proof/permission inventory.
- Weeks 3–4: publish first two case studies; build 40 qualified prospects.
- Weeks 5–6: launch lighting comparison or viewing-distance asset; send first 10 personalized pitches.
- Weeks 7–9: second case-study pair; follow up once; add partner/supplier requests where genuine.
- Weeks 10–13: third case-study pair; second pitch angle; evaluate relevance, replies, placements and assisted conversions.

## Stop conditions

Stop or reject a tactic when it requires fake local presence, fabricated credentials, undisclosed compensation, controlled “independent” reviews, mass forum spam, irrelevant guest posting, private-blog networks, exact-anchor guarantees or publication without customer/media rights.
