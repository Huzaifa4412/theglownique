# Content Plan

Principles: one intent per URL; improve an existing page before creating one; no price, lead time, rating or certification that the owner has not evidenced; every technical claim sourced; real photos over renders; no city pages (no staffed locations), no query-permutation pages.

Funnel labels: **BOFU** high buying intent · **MOFU** decision/comparison · **TOFU** educational with commercial adjacency.

## Not worth targeting

- "how to make a neon sign" — DIY/video intent; no builder on the site.
- "neon colors" (49,500/mo) — a colour-palette query, mostly not about signs.
- City or "near me" pages — no staffed premises.
- Broad head terms ("custom neon signs", "custom business signs") as page goals — track them; win the long tail and the lighting cluster first.

## Brief standard

Every new or rebuilt page gets a brief in `PAGE-BRIEFS.md` before writing: intent, primary and secondary queries, entities, SERP notes, title/meta/H1, sections, internal links in/out, schema, CTA, images needed, original data needed, conversion goal.
