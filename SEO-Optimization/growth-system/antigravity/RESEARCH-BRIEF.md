# Owner's Research Brief (2026-09-28)

This is the owner's research checklist, condensed only by removing repetition. Every section and item is kept.
Read it together with `SPEC.md` §3 and §4, which override it where they conflict: no city or "near me" pages, no shipping or price modifiers stated as facts, only products in the catalog, one English site for all markets, first-party evidence only, and extend the existing knowledge base instead of redoing it.

---

## 1. Role
Senior SEO strategist, competitor intelligence analyst, AEO/GEO strategist, technical SEO expert, content architect, CRO specialist and e-commerce growth consultant. Perform a deep, evidence-based analysis of TheGlownique.com and its competitors, then build a practical SEO/AEO/GEO growth plan that can be implemented on the website.

## 2. Business context
- Website: https://theglownique.com/
- Sells custom LED neon signs, backlit metal signs, acrylic signs, illuminated logo and business signs, wall signs and other custom signage (see `SPEC.md` §1 for what the catalog actually contains).
- Primary market USA; secondary Canada, UK, Australia and other English-speaking buyers where commercially relevant.
- Custom-made to order online: design, text/logo, size, material, lighting type, colour and other specs.
- Commercial intent: custom neon, LED neon, custom business signs, backlit metal signs, illuminated logo signs, acrylic/metal letters, restaurant/cafe, salon/barber/shop, wedding/event, home decor, personalized signs.
- Treat it as a real e-commerce business, not a generic information site.

## 3. Research objectives
1. Real organic-search competitors. 2. Strongest commercial competitors. 3. Who dominates Google for important keywords. 4. Who appears in AI answers and conversational search. 5. Which competitor pages attract rankings, traffic, links, citations and commercial intent. 6. Page types competitors create. 7. Topics, keywords, entities, questions and use cases they cover. 8. Opportunities The Glownique is missing. 9. New pages to create. 10. Existing pages to improve, merge, redirect or remove. 11. Structure for SEO + AEO + GEO + conversions. 12. Topical authority in custom signage. 13. Becoming citable by AI search. 14. Growth without thin or doorway pages.
No generic advice: tie everything to The Glownique, its products, customers, competitors and search opportunities.

## 4. Tools
Use every relevant skill, MCP and tool: Google, Bing, SERP research, image and shopping results, AI-search research, crawling, content extraction, backlink and keyword research, technical SEO, schema/entity analysis and validation, sitemap/robots, internal links, Core Web Vitals, Search Console and analytics, competitor comparison, uploaded files. If a source is unavailable, say so.

## 5. Competitor research
Categories: A direct sellers; B SERP competitors; C marketplaces (Etsy and others); D authority/informational sites. Markets: USA, Canada, UK, Australia, global English. Prioritize by relevance, not fame.
For each major competitor: domain, positioning, product categories, customer segments, markets, price positioning (public only), product options, USPs, page/category/product structure, blog and FAQ strategy, internal linking, schema, reviews and social proof, trust signals, shipping, warranty, customization process, quote/mockup process, conversion elements, CTAs, images, video, backlinks, keyword footprint, content gaps, intent coverage, AEO visibility, GEO/entity visibility. Explain why pages succeed, not only what they contain.

## 6. SERP and keyword intelligence
- Product: custom neon sign(s), custom LED neon sign, personalized neon sign, custom lighted sign, custom backlit sign, backlit metal sign, illuminated logo sign, custom acrylic sign, custom metal letters, LED business sign, custom shop sign.
- Industry/use case: restaurant, cafe, salon, barber shop, gym, hotel, wedding, event, office logo, retail store, brewery, church, home bar, gaming room.
- Design/style: logo neon, name neon, script/cursive neon, custom quote neon, neon logo, backlit logo sign, 3D illuminated letters, acrylic letters, halo-lit letters.
- Questions: cost, lifespan, LED vs glass, mounting, cleaning, size, safety, waterproof, electricity use, installation.
- Modifiers: buy, order, online, USA, near me, custom, cheap, affordable, wholesale, business, personalized, made to order, free shipping, fast shipping (research signals only; see `SPEC.md` §3.3).
Group by intent, topic, funnel stage, product, audience, location, use case, question type, semantic relationship and commercial value. Identify high-value commercial terms, high-conversion long tail, low-competition opportunities, topical-authority terms, question, AEO and GEO/entity opportunities.

## 7. Competitor page-type analysis
Check for: homepage, category, product, material, style, industry, use-case, solution, collection, size, colour, installation, comparison, buying guide, FAQ, glossary, educational, cost/pricing, shipping, warranty, customization guide, inspiration, gallery/project, case study, location, brand/entity, review, supporting content. Identify which types have real visibility, and which The Glownique should and should not build, and why.

## 8. Recommended architecture
Design the actual hierarchy (e.g. Home → Custom Neon → Backlit → Acrylic → Metal → Letters → Business → Industries → Styles → Resources → FAQ) from evidence. For every page type: URL, title, primary and secondary keywords, intent, audience, business objective, content sections, internal links, supporting pages, schema, AEO and GEO opportunities, CTA, priority.

## 9. Page roadmap
Tier 1 highest priority; Tier 2 high opportunity; Tier 3 topical authority; Tier 4 experiments. For each: why it exists, target keyword and intent, why competitors succeed with similar pages, intent type, what makes it unique, links, index or not, canonical/supporting/collection/article. Only pages with standalone value.

## 10. Cannibalization and thin pages
Identify keyword cannibalization, duplicates, near-duplicate industry pages, doorway/location pages, thin collections, excessive variations, duplicate product/category intent, overlapping posts, excessive city pages, low-value AI content. For each proposed page decide: own URL, merge, section, filter, internal link, or noindex. Quality over page count.

## 11. AEO
Research pre-purchase questions (what is it, cost, lifespan, size, logo to neon, LED vs glass, safety, energy, wall mounting, business use, colours and fonts, production time, shipping, waterproof, outdoor use). Decide placement (product, category, FAQ, buying guide, comparison, article). Answers direct, factual, extractable, structured, concise, with depth behind them. Schema only where eligible: Product, Offer, Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, Article, HowTo, LocalBusiness if justified, Review/AggregateRating only if genuine.

## 12. GEO
Make The Glownique a trusted, entity-rich source AI systems can reference: entity clarity, company info, product definitions, expertise, source credibility, factual consistency, brand mentions, third-party references, reviews, citations, original research, statistics, first-party expertise, specs, transparent policies, business identity, structured data, topical coverage, semantic relationships, external authority. Formats: definitions, comparison tables, concise answers, original statistics, buying guides, specs, installation info, real customer examples, case studies, transparent product info, expert explanations, FAQs. Explain the SEO and AI benefit of each.

Find inconsistencies in name, descriptions, URLs, contact info, business details, terminology, social profiles and references. Recommend an entity-consistency strategy.

Decide by intent + business value + content quality + competitive opportunity.

## 24. The central question
What information architecture, commercial pages, topical content, entities, evidence, internal links, structured data and authority signals should The Glownique build so that Google, other search engines, AI answer engines and customers understand it as a highly relevant source for custom neon and custom illuminated signage? Inspect the current site and competitive landscape first, then build recommendations from the evidence.
