## 1. Market Need & SERP Opportunity

A comprehensive scan of Google Page 1 for `"backlit signage"`, `"light box sign"`, and comparison queries revealed a massive content gap:

1. **The False Binary:** Existing competitor articles (e.g. Signarama, Signs.com, Impact Signs, Custom Neon) treat this as an "either/or" commodity question, typically comparing 100mm deep exterior fluorescent box cabinets against front-lit acrylic letters.
2. **Missing Optical Physics & Wall Interaction:** Zero ranking articles explain how halo backlighting uses the wall as an optical component (reflectance, finish, texture), or how edge-lit ultra-thin lightboxes use laser-etched dot patterns and light guide plates (LGP) to turn edge light into uniform face illumination.
3. **The Standoff & Installation Blindspot:** Competitors offer zero guidance on standoff distances (20–40mm), power supply (driver) concealment, Class 2 12V low-voltage wiring, or ADA §307.2 4-inch protrusion limits for interior corridors.
4. **The Hybrid Reality:** In luxury retail and hospitality, businesses do not choose one or the other. They place a halo-lit brand sign on the feature wall (where the brand lives and photographs without camera glare) and ultra-thin lightboxes where information changes (menu boards, pricing, seasonal campaigns).
