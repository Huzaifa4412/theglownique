## 3. Search Intent & E-E-A-T Architecture

Searchers querying `"channel letter signs"` fall into two distinct segments:
1. **Commercial decision-makers (Storefront owners, restaurateurs, franchise operators):** Need to know what styles exist, whether their landlord's criteria package allows flush mounting or requires a raceway, how weatherproofing works, and what lighting color temperature (3000K vs 4000K vs 6500K) matches their brand.
2. **General searchers & architects:** Seeking a definitive, clear definition of the anatomy of a channel letter (face, returns, backing, trim cap, LEDs).

### Information Gain Built Into This Post:
- **Exact Material Specifications:** ASTM B209 sheet aluminum (.063" to .090"), marine-grade 304/316 stainless steel, 3/16" impact-resistant cast acrylic faces, CAB Jewelite trim caps.
- **USSC Viewing Distance Formula:** 1 inch of letter height per 25-30 feet of viewing distance at night for illuminated channel letters.
- **Raceway vs. Flush Engineering:** Explanation of building wall penetrations (3-4 anchors for a raceway vs. 3-5 through-wall penetrations per individual letter for flush mount).
- **Code Compliance:** UL 48 standards, NEC Article 600, Class 2 12V/24V low-voltage power drivers.
- **Zero Pricing Violations:** Strict adherence to `lib/claims.ts` by explaining cost *drivers* (letter height, stroke complexity, lighting style, mounting method) without publishing speculative dollar figures, routing budget inquiries to `/guides/channel-letter-sign-cost`.
