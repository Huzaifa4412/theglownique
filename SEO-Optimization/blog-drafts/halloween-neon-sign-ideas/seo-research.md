## Editorial decision

Write **9 Halloween Neon Sign Ideas for Parties, Cafes and Cozy Corners**. The audience is home hosts and small hospitality/retail owners looking for seasonal display inspiration. The next step is a custom design inquiry.

The evidence supports a timely seasonal opportunity, not a claim that neon signs are viral in 2026. NRF's September 1, 2026 article reports early Halloween shopping, and Ubersuggest's available keyword history shows a September/October 2025 peak. The full 2026 NRF Halloween consumer survey was still scheduled for late September when checked. No 2026 spending forecast is asserted in the article.

The nine concepts, color combinations and staging suggestions are original editorial recommendations. They are not presented as measured trend rankings, customer installations or first-hand testing.

No unsupported sales lift, lifespan, power-saving percentage, universal weatherproofing or hands-on experience claims are included.
