# SEO & Search Intent Research: Custom Neon Sign Lead Times & Delivery

## 1. Search Intent & Commercial Context
- **Target Query:** `how long does a custom neon sign take`
- **Secondary Intent:** `custom neon sign lead time`, `how long do neon signs take to make`, `custom neon sign turnaround time`
- **Search Intent:** Informational + Commercial Investigation (MOFU / BOFU). Buyers have an upcoming event (wedding, storefront grand opening, trade show, holiday) and are calculating whether an order placed today will arrive in time.
- **Pain Points:** Fear of late arrival, fear of hidden rush fees, lack of transparency regarding manufacturing vs shipping time, broken signs on arrival from rushed un-tested fabrication.

## 2. Competitive Deficiencies Mapped
- Competitor articles give vague ranges ("1 to 4 weeks") without breaking down vector mockup approval, CNC milling, assembly, QC burn-in, and courier transit.
- Competitors fail to explain the critical 48-hour burn-in quality test, which is why cheap rush signs frequently arrive DOA.
- Competitors charge 30%–50% rush fees; The Glownique provides priority workshop scheduling at no extra fee when the event date is provided.
- Competitors do not provide an actionable Event Countdown Matrix for weddings, storefronts, and trade shows.

## 3. Position 0 Direct Answer Architecture
- **Snippet Formulation:** "A custom LED neon sign takes 7 to 14 business days from mockup approval to delivery, broken into 3 to 5 business days for precision CNC fabrication and 3 to 8 business days for express air shipping. Commercial metal channel letters and lightboxes require 10 to 18 business days. Rush orders can be fast-tracked to 7 to 9 days at no extra fee when your event date is specified."
