Rush orders can be fast-tracked to 7 to 9 days at no extra fee when your event date is specified.

Every millimeter of acrylic is routed to your specific typography, every silicone flex segment is bent and soldered by hand, and every electrical circuit must be burn-in tested for safety.

## The 4 phases of custom neon sign production

To understand where your sign is at any given moment, it helps to examine the four sequential stages of bespoke illuminated manufacturing:

### Phase 1: Design digitization and vector mockup (within 24 hours)

The production journey begins the moment you submit your text, brand logo, or graphic artwork:

1. **Vector Artwork Generation:** Our design team digitizes your submission into precision vector cutlines. If you submitted a raster bitmap (such as a PNG, JPEG, or phone screenshot), technicians manually trace each letterform, ensuring curves are continuous and uniform.
2. **Backboard Cutline Engineering:** We generate the exact outline for the acrylic mounting backboard—whether cut-to-shape (contour cut following the letters), whole rectangle, or hollow cutout. Browse our guide on [how much do custom neon signs cost](/blog/how-much-do-custom-neon-signs-cost) for detailed comparisons of acrylic cut styles.
3. **Component & Electrical Mapping:** The engineering draft establishes wire exit points, silicone bend radii, power adapter amperage sizing, and mounting hole positions.
4. **Client Review and Approval:** We deliver a high-resolution digital mockup within 24 hours, seven days a week (including weekends). Under The Glownique's standing policy, all revisions are completely free and unlimited. The official manufacturing timeline begins the moment you give final sign-off.

### Phase 2: CNC milling and artisan hand-assembly (3 to 5 business days)

Once the vector file is approved, it moves to the physical workshop floor:

1. **Precision Acrylic CNC Milling:** A high-speed CNC router cuts 5mm optical-grade cast acrylic sheeting to match your approved backboard profile with sub-millimeter precision. Outer edges are flame-polished to produce crystal-clear glass-like clarity.
2. **Channel Routing & Positioning:** The CNC machine cuts shallow guide tracks into the acrylic face. These tracks define the exact path where the flexible silicone tubing will sit, preventing optical distortion or uneven letter spacing.
3. **Hand-Forming Silicone LED Flex:** Unlike vintage rigid glass neon that required open gas fires, modern signs use flexible, optical-grade silicone extrusion packed with high-density SMD LED diodes. Skilled artisans hand-bend and seat the silicone flex into the milled acrylic channels.
4. **Micro-Soldering Wire Harnesses:** Each cut section of neon flex is hand-soldered to insulated copper wire jumpers using 60/40 rosin-core solder following IPC/WHMA-A-620 Class 2 standards. Every connection is sealed with heat-shrink tubing and transparent silicone potting to prevent moisture corrosion.
5. **Dimensional Metal & Lightbox Differences:** For 3D metal channel letters or ultra-thin lightboxes, fabrication expands to 3 to 10 business days. Channel letters require laser-cutting 304 stainless steel faces, hand-welding metal returns, and installing internal LED modules. Review our comprehensive [channel letter signs guide](/blog/types-of-channel-letter-signs) to explore commercial storefront letter fabrication.

### Phase 3: The mandatory 48-hour QC burn-in test (days 4 to 5)

Many budget online sellers rush signs out the door minutes after the last wire is soldered. This shortcut is the number-one reason cheap signs arrive dead on arrival (DOA) or develop erratic flickers within weeks:

1. **Continuous Full-Load Burn-In:** In our workshop, every completed sign is mounted on a testing wall and connected to its designated 12V Class 2 power supply for 48 continuous hours.
2. **Thermal Equilibrium Verification:** The test checks that the power adapter, inline PWM dimmer, and internal copper solder joints maintain stable operating temperatures under continuous load. Read our technical analysis on [can you leave a neon sign on all the time](/blog/can-you-leave-a-neon-sign-on-all-the-time) to see how thermal equilibrium protects driver longevity.
3. **Voltage Drop and Luminous Consistency:** Technicians inspect the entire sign for voltage sag—ensuring the first letter and the last letter glow with identical lumen output and zero color shifting.
4. **Wireless RF Dimmer Testing:** The included wireless remote dimmer is paired and tested through ten brightness levels and dynamic modes. If you ever experience electrical interference on an existing sign, consult our troubleshooting guide on [why is my neon sign flickering](/blog/why-is-my-neon-sign-flickering).

### Phase 4: Custom crating and tracked express air transit (3 to 8 business days)

Once the sign passes its 48-hour quality audit, it enters the logistics department:

1. **Multi-Layer Protective Wrapping:** The sign is encased in peelable protective film, followed by multiple wraps of high-density expanded polyethylene (EPE) shock-absorbing foam.
2. **Reinforced Crating:** Custom signs are packed in heavy-duty double-wall honeycomb cardboard cartons or custom timber-reinforced shipping crates with rigid corner protectors.
3. **Hardware & Accessory Inclusion:** Every box includes the certified 12V power supply with regional wall plug, wireless remote dimmer, stainless steel mounting standoffs, and drywall anchors.
4. **Air Courier Dispatch:** Shipments are handed directly to premier express air carriers (DHL Express, FedEx, or UPS). Customers receive automated real-time tracking numbers the moment the courier scans the parcel manifest.
5. **Standing Free Shipping Policy:** The Glownique provides free worldwide delivery on all custom signage orders as a standing policy with no hidden customs fees or freight surcharges.

## Event planning: when should you place your order?

A missed event deadline is a total failure, regardless of how beautiful the sign looks when it finally arrives. To eliminate stress, follow these recommended planning windows:

### 1. Weddings and social receptions (order 3 to 4 weeks prior)

For wedding receptions, anniversary parties, and milestone birthdays, order three to four weeks before the event date:

1. **Why the Buffer Matters:** While your sign will typically arrive in 10 to 12 days, a 3-week window provides a comfortable buffer. You have ample time to unbox the sign, test the wireless remote, coordinate with your florist or wedding planner, and test-hang the sign on your photo arch or greenery wall.
2. **Photography Lighting Checks:** Having the sign early allows your photographer to test camera shutter speeds and adjust dimming levels, avoiding rolling shutter banding on your special day.
3. **Wedding Sign Inspiration:** Explore our dedicated collection of handcrafted [wedding neon signs](/custom-signage/wedding-signs) for popular surname monograms, romantic script phrases, and freestanding cocktail bar accents.

### 2. Commercial storefronts and restaurants (order 4 to 5 weeks prior)

Opening a brick-and-mortar storefront or launching a new restaurant requires coordination across multiple contractors:

1. **Contractor & Electrician Coordination:** Commercial illuminated signs—especially exterior channel letters or facade lightboxes—require building permits, dedicated 120V junction boxes, and masonry anchors.
2. **Inspection Sign-Off:** Having the sign delivered two weeks before grand opening ensures your licensed sign installer can mount the letters, fish low-voltage wires through the drywall, and secure municipal electrical inspection sign-off without delaying opening day.
3. **Commercial Logo Capabilities:** View our custom [business logo neon signs](/business-signs/custom-logo-neon-signs) to see how corporate branding is adapted into illuminated signage.

### 3. Trade shows, expos, and corporate activations (order 3 to 4 weeks prior)

Exhibiting at convention centers like the Las Vegas Convention Center, McCormick Place, or Orlando Orange County involves strict logistics:

1. **Advance Warehouse Marshaling:** Convention drayage contractors require booth freight to arrive at the advance warehouse up to two weeks prior to the show opening. Shipping directly to the show floor during move-in frequently incurs heavy surcharges and risk of delivery delays.
2. **Flight Case and Packing:** Ordering a month ahead allows your marketing team to inspect the sign, verify universal power compatibility, and pack it safely into road cases alongside exhibition booths. Review our commercial guide for [trade show booth signs](/business-signs/trade-show-signs).

## What accelerates vs delays your neon sign order?

Understanding the primary variables that impact production speed allows you to avoid unnecessary turnaround bottlenecks:

### Factors that speed up your order:

1. **Submitting Clean Vector Artwork:** Providing high-resolution vector files (.AI, .EPS, .SVG, or editable .PDF) allows our CAD technicians to generate CNC toolpaths instantly, shaving 24 to 48 hours off pre-production.
2. **Fast Mockup Approval:** Promptly reviewing and approving your digital proof ensures your order moves directly into the next morning's CNC milling queue.
3. **Specifying Your Event Date:** Always enter your target event date in the order notes or chat with our design team. Our workshop production scheduler automatically prioritizes upcoming event dates across the fabrication queue.
4. **Accurate Delivery Details:** Providing a direct mobile telephone number and full street address (never a P.O. Box) ensures express courier dispatch without customs or address verification holds.

### Factors that can add production days:

1. **Complex Multi-Layered Graphics:** Highly intricate multi-colored logos with overlapping silicone channels or embedded UV-printed acrylic gradients require meticulous manual soldering and multi-pass CNC engraving, which can add 1 to 2 business days.
2. **Outdoor IP67 Weatherproofing:** Signs rated for exterior outdoor use undergo full silicone injection potting. The vulcanizing silicone requires 24 hours of room-temperature curing before the sign can be safely handled and packaged.
3. **Repeated Mockup Revisions:** While revisions are always free and unlimited, each design adjustment requires a new vector proof, which pauses production until approval is received.
4. **Peak Holiday Logistics (Q4 Surges):** During peak holiday periods (Halloween, Black Friday, and Christmas), international air freight hubs experience unprecedented cargo volume. Ordering at least one week earlier during November and December is strongly recommended.
