## 1. Market Need & SERP Opportunity

A comprehensive scan of Google Page 1 and Reddit (`r/NeonSigns`, `r/Filmmakers`) for `"neon sign flickering"` and `"why is my neon sign flickering"` revealed clear commercial intent and systemic competitor vulnerabilities:

1. **The Glass vs. LED Neon Confusion:** Over 85% of consumers searching for neon sign repair or flickering diagnosis own modern 12V LED silicone neon flex signs, yet ranking guides are either written for vintage 15,000V glass neon tubes or provide generic, unhelpful 300-word fluff with no electrical testing steps.
2. **Missing 5-Minute Component Isolation (The Bypass Test):** Not a single leading competitor website details the simple direct bypass test: unplugging inline dimmers and remote receivers to immediately isolate whether the failure is in the controller, the power adapter, or the silicone flex itself.
3. **The 80% Driver Headroom Rule:** Most cheap imported signs ship with power supplies pushed to 95–100% of their rated wattage. When operated for longer than an hour, these adapters overheat and trip their internal thermal cut-off switches, resulting in cyclical strobing or shutdown that users mistakenly blame on the sign itself.
4. **Camera / Video Shutter Banding (PWM Conflict):** Event planners, wedding venues, and content creators frequently believe their signs are broken because they flicker or produce dark rolling bands when recorded on smartphones. Explaining pulse-width modulation (PWM) frequency and camera shutter angle calibration solves this immediate pain point without hardware replacement.
5. **High Commercial Economics:** The keyword cluster features CPCs up to $4.59 to $6.91, demonstrating that users searching this query are ready to purchase replacement power adapters, dimmers, or upgrade to a professional commercial-grade custom sign from The Glownique.
