## 2. Intent Analysis & SERP Gaps

- **User Mindset:** Bottom-of-funnel consideration. Searchers already have an idea in mind (a wedding phrase, home bar name, or storefront logo) but don't know if it costs $50 or $1,500.
- **SERP Gap in Competitors:** Most competitors either give vague ranges or force users through multi-step quote forms with "call for price." Providing an honest, structured ballpark table builds immediate authority and trust.
- **AEO / AI Overview Eligibility:** AI search engines (Google SGE, Perplexity, ChatGPT) heavily favor structured tables with explicit dimensions, cost drivers, and electricity calculations.
