# How Much Do Custom Neon Signs Cost? (2026 Price & Sizing Breakdown)

A custom LED neon sign typically costs between **$120 and $1,200+**, depending primarily on its overall dimensions, total letter count, and design complexity. A compact single-word bedroom sign usually costs **$120 to $220**, a medium two-word wedding or photo backdrop ranges from **$260 to $480**, and large commercial storefront logos start around **$550 to $950+**.

Because every custom sign is built to order from scratch, manufacturers rarely display a single fixed price tag. Instead, your final quote reflects the exact physical meters of flexible silicone LED tubing required, the laser-cut acrylic backboard dimensions, and any specialised electrical accessories. This guide breaks down realistic 2026 pricing across standard sizes, outlines the five main factors that determine your quote, and reveals how much electricity an LED neon sign actually consumes.

This provides enough length for elegant script cursive without crowding the letters, while remaining light enough to mount easily on drywall or a freestanding floral frame.

## The five factors that actually determine your quote

When you send a sketch, font choice, or vector file into our workshop, our design team calculates your quote based on five specific physical factors:

### 1. Letter count and total tubing length

In neon fabrication, you do not pay strictly per letter as you might with engraved metal; you pay for the **total linear length of LED silicone flex** and the number of individual soldered connections required.

A four-letter word like *LOVE* in a flowing script font uses roughly 1.2 meters of continuous silicone flex. A twelve-letter phrase like *HAPPILY EVER AFTER* across two lines might require 4.5 meters of tubing and over twenty distinct electrical jumper joints. Each cut, curve, and connection point demands skilled hand-assembly and bench testing, making longer phrases proportionally more labor-intensive.

### 2. Physical dimensions and acrylic backing footprint

Acrylic sheet is the rigid backbone of every LED neon sign. We use premium high-clarity 5 mm to 8 mm cast acrylic that does not warp or yellow under lighting heat.

As a sign scales from 24 inches to 60 inches wide, the required acrylic sheet area more than quadruples. Larger acrylic plates also demand sturdier wooden crate packaging, higher volumetric shipping freight, and thicker standoff hardware to ensure the sign arrives completely undamaged.

### 3. Backboard cut style: cut-to-shape vs rectangular

How the acrylic backing is trimmed affects both aesthetics and manufacturing cost:

1. **Cut to Shape (Contour):** The laser cutter follows the gentle outer outline of your words with a smooth 1-inch border. This is our most popular and versatile finish, combining structural strength with minimal visual footprint.
2. **Cut to Letter (Hollow):** The acrylic is laser-routed closely around each individual character, removing negative space between words. This creates a floating appearance on exposed brick or dark paint, but requires higher laser machine time and delicate manual handling.
3. **Full Rectangular Slab:** The neon sits on a solid geometric sheet of clear, glossy black, or mirror acrylic. It is structurally rigid and ideal for free-standing tabletop displays or signs incorporating UV-printed subtext.

![Diagram showing cut to shape, cut to letter, and rectangular acrylic backboard cut styles](/blog/how-much-do-custom-neon-signs-cost/backboard-cut-styles-cost.webp "The three primary acrylic backboard cut styles: contour cut to shape, cut to letter, and full rectangle.")

### 4. Color count and multi-color LED flex

A single-color sign in classic warm white, cool white, blush pink, or electric cyan runs on a uniform circuit. If your design mixes three distinct neon colors—such as a green monstera leaf behind pink cursive text—each color section requires independent wire routing, voltage balancing, and distinct silicone tubing strips. Multi-color artwork adds between 15% and 30% to baseline fabrication costs due to the additional benchwork.

If you desire full color-changing RGB capabilities where the sign cycles through colors via smartphone app or wireless remote, expect an additional $50 to $120 for digital addressable chips and an RF controller module.

### 5. Indoor standard vs IP67 outdoor weatherproofing

Standard indoor neon signs are designed for climate-controlled rooms, venues, and shop interiors. When a sign is specified for exterior installation—such as an outdoor patio, building facade, or food truck—every electrical contact must be encased in silicone sealant and encapsulated against moisture.

An **IP67 weatherproof upgrade** includes three essential treatments:

1. **Potted silicone end-caps:** Moisture-sealed terminal joints that resist heavy rain, snow, and condensation.
2. **UV-stabilised exterior flex:** Outdoor-grade silicone jacketing that will not discolor or crack under direct sunlight.
3. **Weatherproof power supply:** An outdoor-rated IP67 driver with sealed screw-tight barrel connectors.

This weatherproofing treatment typically adds 20% to 25% to the base price of the sign, but ensures years of outdoor operation without short circuits.

## LED neon vs traditional glass neon: initial and lifetime cost

Many buyers compare modern LED neon with traditional gas-discharge glass neon. While hand-blown glass neon possesses historical romance, it is substantially more expensive to purchase, ship, and maintain:

| Feature / Cost factor | Modern LED neon flex | Traditional gas glass neon |
|---|---|---|
| Typical purchase price (30" sign) | **$280 – $380** | **$700 – $1,400+** |
| Power supply voltage | Safe low-voltage 12V DC | High-voltage 3,000V – 15,000V AC |
| Fragility & shipping risk | Virtually unbreakable silicone & acrylic | Fragile hollow glass tubes prone to transit breakage |
| Typical operational lifespan | 50,000+ hours (over 10 years at 12 hrs/day) | 20,000 to 40,000 hours before gas refill |
| Replacement / repair cost | Inexpensive plug-and-play adapter replacement | Requires specialist local glass-blowing workshop |

## Do custom neon signs use a lot of electricity?

One of the most frequent pre-purchase hesitations is whether leaving a neon sign glowing all night will drive up your monthly electric bill. The short answer is: **virtually not at all**.

Modern LED neon operates on a safe, energy-efficient 12-volt DC transformer. A standard 30-inch sign consumes approximately **35 to 55 watts**—roughly equivalent to a single modest household LED light bulb.

For a typical 45-watt sign illuminated 10 hours daily, total energy consumption is approximately 0.45 kWh per day, or 13.5 kWh per month. At the US national commercial average electricity rate of $0.16 per kilowatt-hour from the US Energy Information Administration, the total running cost comes out to **$2.16 per month**—less than eight cents per day.

By comparison, a vintage glass neon sign of comparable physical size draws between 250 and 450 watts through a heavy iron-core transformer, costing $15 to $25 per month in electricity and throwing off noticeable ambient heat. LED neon remains completely cool to the touch after eighteen hours of continuous illumination.

## Five practical ways to lower your custom neon sign quote

If you have a set budget for your project, small design adjustments can significantly reduce manufacturing costs without sacrificing visual impact:

1. **Keep text to one or two lines:** Taller, multi-line signs require larger square acrylic sheets and multiple electrical jumpers. Formatting words horizontally across a single line usually reduces the overall footprint and price.
2. **Choose clean, open fonts:** Elaborate calligraphy with intricate swirls increases linear tubing length and tight hand-bends. Clean modern cursive or geometric sans-serif fonts require less flex tubing and take less bench time to fabricate.
3. **Select a single signature color:** Designing your sign in one cohesive hue such as Warm White, Ice Blue, or Lemon Yellow eliminates color-matching splices and keeps fabrication straightforward.
4. **Use dimming instead of downsizing:** If you are worried a sign might look too intense in a small bedroom or office, do not sacrifice physical size. Order the correct legibility dimensions and add an inline dimmer remote so you can soften the intensity down to 10% brightness for evening ambiance.
5. **Request a true-to-scale digital mockup first:** Never order from a low-resolution automated preview. A professional workshop will map your exact vector letters to the centimeter, ensuring you do not pay for wasted acrylic margin.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why are custom neon signs priced higher than generic mass-produced Amazon signs?
Mass-produced signs sold on discount marketplaces are molded in fixed factory batches of thousands using thin plastic molds and low-grade LEDs that often dim or flicker within six months. A true custom neon sign is made to order: your specific words or logo are laser-cut onto architectural acrylic, hand-shaped with commercial-grade silicone flex, hand-soldered, bench-tested for 24 hours, and backed by a comprehensive multi-year warranty.

### Do custom neon signs cost per letter or per inch?
While some online calculators use rough per-letter rules of thumb, reputable workshops price signs by total physical dimensions, linear silicone flex length, and complexity. A four-letter word in 18-inch script requires far more material than eight letters in compact 4-inch print.

### Is shipping included in custom neon sign pricing?
Because custom acrylic signs require reinforced protective packaging and wooden corner bracing, shipping rates depend on box dimensions. Reputable manufacturers provide fully tracked door-to-door express delivery with transit insurance. At The Glownique, every custom sign ships fully tracked worldwide with customs clearance support.

### Are outdoor waterproof neon signs safe in freezing winter temperatures?
Yes. Quality outdoor LED neon signs engineered with silicone flex jackets maintain elasticity from -20°C up to 50°C. Unlike glass neon tubes that struggle to ignite in cold weather, solid-state LEDs illuminate instantly at full brightness in sub-zero conditions.

### Can I get an exact quote and preview before paying?
Yes, and you should always insist on one. You should never be asked to commit payment before seeing a scaled digital mockup of your sign with exact dimensions, color rendering, and wire placement confirmed.
