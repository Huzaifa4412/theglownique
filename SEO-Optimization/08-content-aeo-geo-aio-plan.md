# Content, AEO, GEO and AIO Plan

## Publishing principle

Create the best decision evidence for a B2B signage buyer. Clear headings, direct answers, tables and FAQs are useful because people can navigate them and multiple systems can extract them—not because of an assumed AI formatting trick.

Google's current guidance (`G-AI-01`) prioritizes indexed, non-commodity, expert-led, people-first content and explicitly rejects the need for AI-only rewrites, tiny “chunks” or special files. Cross-platform citation work therefore layers extractability and monitoring onto strong SEO rather than creating a separate content corpus.

## First 12 brief approval gate

Use [templates/content-brief.md](templates/content-brief.md). A brief is approved only when it contains:

- one primary intent and canonical URL from the keyword map;
- named audience and decision to be made;
- unique information gain unavailable from generic summaries;
- source plan and expert reviewer;
- required first-party proof and rights status;
- internal-link inputs/outputs;
- visible commercial facts and claim-register references;
- conversion action and analytics event;
- schema eligibility decision, including “none” when appropriate;
- refresh trigger and owner.

## Page quality contract

Every page must:

1. answer the page's central question or value proposition near the beginning;
2. add first-party or expert value: real media, measurements, project constraints, materials, manufacturing insight, approved price ranges, lead times, installation context or outcomes;
3. distinguish verified facts, sourced general guidance and recommendations;
4. show author/reviewer identity, relevant experience, publish/modified dates and sources;
5. use descriptive headings, semantic HTML, useful tables/lists and captions where they improve decisions;
6. explain limitations and cases where another product/approach is better;
7. expose specifications, price basis, delivery and contact paths in server-rendered HTML;
8. link within the cluster and to a relevant proof asset;
9. include a specific free-mockup/quote action rather than a generic CTA;
10. pass the on-page QA template.

No universal word count applies. Depth is measured by decision completeness and evidence.

## AEO/extractability patterns

Use only when they naturally fit the reader's task:

- a concise definition after a “What is…” heading;
- a balanced comparison table for “X vs Y” decisions;
- numbered steps for a real process;
- cost-driver table with units, date and scope;
- FAQs that answer actual sales questions;
- labeled diagrams and photos for lighting/mounting concepts;
- summary and recommendation criteria for complex pages.

Do not manufacture arbitrary 40–60-word blocks, repeat the same answer in several formats or add FAQ schema merely to chase a retired Google rich result.

## Media as searchable evidence

For every new asset, store:

- descriptive file name and stable URL;
- product/project reference and rights status;
- factual alt text; empty alt when decorative;
- caption explaining what the image proves;
- dimensions, material, lighting style and context when known;
- creator/source and capture date;
- thumbnail/poster and transcript or description for meaningful video;
- no `VideoObject` unless all visible eligibility fields are accurate.

Prioritize original before/after, same-logo lighting comparisons, fabrication steps, installation context and dimensional diagrams over generic lifestyle imagery.

## Pricing content rule

Pricing is powerful decision content and high-risk structured data. Before publishing any range:

1. define the included sign configuration, size/unit, options, shipping, tax/duties and installation scope;
2. use recent completed quotes/orders and document the sample/method;
3. obtain operations approval and an effective date;
4. display cost drivers and reasons a final quote varies;
5. schedule monthly validation;
6. synchronize visible page, metadata, schema, Etsy references and `llms.txt`.

If no defensible range is available, publish cost drivers and sample scope methodology—not invented numbers.

## Editorial workflow

`brief → evidence/permissions → expert interview → draft → fact/claim review → SEO/internal-link review → legal/safety review where relevant → media/alt/caption → preview QA → publish → IndexNow/sitemap → 7/14-day index check → 30/60/90-day performance review`

Hard stops:

- unsupported claim or price;
- no unique proof for an industry/case page;
- customer/media permission unclear;
- materially overlapping intent;
- schema does not match visible content;
- policy/privacy conflict.

## Refresh rules

- Volatile commercial facts: validate monthly and immediately after Etsy/promotion/policy changes.
- Pricing guides: monthly review; visible modified date only after material update.
- Product/industry pages: quarterly or when GSC/conversion/product facts change.
- Compliance/install guide: quarterly and after expert/legal updates.
- Case studies: correct errors immediately; otherwise preserve historical facts and add dated updates.
- Pages with impressions but low CTR: review query/title/snippet match after sufficient data.
- Pages with traffic but weak qualified conversion: improve decision proof/CTA before adding more traffic content.
- Near-duplicate/zero-value pages: consolidate rather than endlessly refresh.
