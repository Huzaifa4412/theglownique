# 90-Day Roadmap

Start condition: accountable owners assigned and GSC/analytics access requested.  
Capacity: two expert-reviewed pages per week after quality gates.  
Portfolio target: up to 24 pages by day 90 if publishing begins in week 2; quality and P0 fixes take precedence.

## Outcomes by day 30

- Baselines imported from GSC, analytics, Bing Webmaster Tools and current AI prompt sample.
- All current intended routes classified and `/studio` production handling approved.
- Privacy/tooling, promotion, price, warranty, production/transit, rating and Etsy URL decisions approved.
- Technical P0 work shipped and validated in a separate implementation release.
- First 12 content briefs approved with evidence/owners.
- First six pages published only if the technical release and proof gates pass.

## Outcomes by day 60

- B2B hub and all four B2B product destinations live.
- Priority pricing/comparison guides live with approved evidence.
- At least two detailed case studies live.
- IndexNow and Bing AI Performance baseline operational.
- First linkable asset in production or launched.

## Outcomes by day 90

- Up to 24 high-quality pages live: hub, four products, six industries, seven guides and six case studies.
- Contextual links connect every product, industry, guide, proof and conversion layer.
- Intended new pages have 7/14-day index evidence and an investigation ticket if outside the ≥90% target.
- Non-brand impressions and query coverage are established; no ranking guarantee is attached.
- AI citations, mentions and recommendations are recorded separately.
- First sustained, ethical outreach cycle completed.

## Work-in-progress limits

- Maximum four drafted pages awaiting expert review.
- Maximum two pages awaiting claims/legal review.
- No case study enters draft without permission status.
- No new route starts while a P0 indexability/privacy defect from the same template is open.

## Day-30/60/90 decision meetings

### Day 30

Decide whether index/claims infrastructure is reliable enough to sustain two pages weekly. If not, pause volume and fix the system.

### Day 60

Compare impressions, indexing time, early conversions and sales feedback by cluster. Shift the remaining calendar toward pages with evidence of demand and conversion—not vanity traffic.

### Day 90

Classify every page: scale, refresh, consolidate, wait for data or retire. Approve months 4–6 only after the measurement audit.

## Success and escalation rules

- Target ≥90% of intended pages valid/indexed within 14 days; investigate individually when missed.
- A page with no impressions is not automatically bad at 30 days; check index status, query demand, internal links and evidence before acting.
- A page generating irrelevant inquiries is a targeting failure even if traffic grows.
- A public claim dispute, privacy mismatch or schema manual-action risk is an immediate P0.
- Publishing fewer evidence-rich pages is acceptable; thin substitutes are not.
